THE MANIFESTO

Two Companies Control Your Phone. That Ends Now.

For decades, every smartphone has run one of two operating systems — both controlled by corporations with financial incentives to harvest your data, restrict what you can run, and lock you into their ecosystems.

Your phone — your most personal computer — runs code you cannot inspect, cannot modify, and cannot trust.

Crimson OS exists to change this. Built from scratch on an original kernel. Not Android. Not iOS. Not Linux. A completely independent codebase designed from day one for security, privacy, and user sovereignty.

This is not a modified Android. This is not a Linux phone. This is a third option.

RISKS & HONESTY

What We'll Tell You Straight

This is a software Kickstarter. There is no hardware to manufacture, no supply chain to manage, and no FCC certification required. The kernel is already written and the code is publicly auditable.

The primary risk is time. Porting a kernel to new hardware always takes longer than expected. We are committing to transparency: every development update — including setbacks — will be shared with backers in real time.

We are not selling a finished product. We are inviting you to fund the next phase of something already in motion.
